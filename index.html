Overview

Context-Aware Deep Lagrangian Networks adapt to unknown payloads by inferring a latent context from measurement histories. However, the LSTM encoder they rely on provides no guarantees on sensitivity to measurement disturbances. BoBDeLaN addresses this limitation by combining a contracting, gain-bounded Recurrent Equilibrium Network (REN) with stable linear filtering, enabling frequency-shaped disturbance sensitivity while preserving the Lagrangian structure.

Key contributions and results:

  • Theoretical guarantees: Exponential forgetting of recurrent initial states and frequency-weighted bounds on context and residual torque prediction sensitivity.
  • Frequency-shaped adaptation: Reduced high-frequency sensitivity compared to an LSTM encoder using the same input filter.
  • Improved dynamics prediction: Lower residual torque prediction errors and smoother control across simulated payload variations.
  • Robust hardware control: Real-world pick-and-place experiments demonstrate substantially less performance degradation under injected disturbances compared to the LSTM baseline.

Real Hardware Experiments

Adaptive Control with Changing Payloads

We compare nominal MPC, CaDeLaC with an LSTM encoder, and CaDeLaC with BoBDeLaN on a real Franka Panda performing a pick-and-place task with changing payloads. Both learned controllers improve dynamics prediction, while BoBDeLaN achieves smoother motion and lower residual torque prediction errors.

Adaptive Control with Changing Payloads under Disturbances

We repeat the experiment with injected 5 Hz and 10 Hz disturbances in the encoder's torque measurements. While the CaDeLaC LSTM exhibits substantially degraded tracking and oscillatory motion, BoBDeLaN maintains smoother and more robust control.

CaDeLaC BoBDeLaN: With and Without Disturbances

We compare BoBDeLaN with and without injected measurement disturbances. Its gain-bounded recurrent encoder and low-pass filtering limit the effect of disturbances, resulting in only small degradation in tracking and motion smoothness.

CaDeLaC LSTM: With and Without Disturbances

We compare the original CaDeLaC LSTM with and without the same injected disturbances. Despite using identical input filtering, the LSTM encoder exhibits substantially increased sensitivity, leading to larger tracking errors and more oscillatory motion.
